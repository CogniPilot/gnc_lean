Sampled estimator error bounds

GNC.Estimation.SampledErrorBound proves regional nonlinear sampled-error
containment, invariant tubes, finite GPS-outage growth, GPS-return recovery
and forward-publication bounds. The map certificates are conditional and
uniform over explicit admissible contexts. Accepted and rejected measurement
branches both need certificates; no trajectory containment is assumed in
place of proving it.

GNC.Estimation.LieErrorEnvelope derives finite-time navigation error bounds
from GNC's exact SE_2(3) log equations. Matched bias gives an exact triangular
linear flow. The biased result retains nonlinear tangent-bias forcing with
explicit uniform bounds. Local/right coordinate conjugacy and Euclidean
Cartesian position/velocity reconstruction are proved, including the pose
lever-arm terms required by right-error reconstruction.

GNC.Estimation.GpsDeniedGeometry proves horizontal translation invariance
for ideal flat-ground, spatially uniform-field flow/range/barometer/magnetic
and inertial observations, identical histories, and an estimator-independent
minimax error lower bound. This excludes terrain/landmark/magnetic maps and
any additional absolute-position information from earlier GPS history.

GNC.Estimation.CorrectionErrorBound derives StepBound coefficients for an
adaptive-gain correction from uniform linear, gain, observation, reset and
noise bounds. A separate implementation-defect bound pads the disturbance
budget. Unobserved nonzero directions are preserved by I-KH and cannot
contract at an individual partial observation. These results prevent using
nominal gains, covariance optimality or an exact-real proof as substitutes
for nonlinear, observability or generated-code certificates.

Ordered upper envelopes alone do not establish lower actual error. A separate
uniform-upper/rival-witness theorem states a sufficient strict worst-case
comparison on a common admissible input set. Neither that theorem nor the
local quadratic-versus-cubic comparison instantiates ESKF, native PX4 EKF2 or
native ArduPilot EKF3. Numeric map/forcing/branch certificates, Gaussian event
coverage, compiler/IEEE754 refinement and native-filter superiority remain
open obligations. The existing matched experiments still contain ESKF losses.
