Correlated Kalman correction and inertial aiding observability
============================================================

GNC.Estimation.KalmanCorrection proves exact-real identities for arbitrary
finite-dimensional state and observation spaces. These results apply to an
ESKF, EKF, Schmidt filter, or any other correction with the stated covariance
model. They do not assert superiority of a named flight stack.

Let P=Cov(e), R=Cov(v), C=Cov(e,v), and innovation H e+v.
Then B=P H^T+C and S=H P H^T+H C+C^T H^T+R.
The correction error is (I-KH)e-Kv.

generalized_joseph identifies its covariance with
P-K B^T-B K^T+K S K^T. It requires symmetric P and allows arbitrary K,
including constrained or projected gains. Omitting the cross terms is invalid
when observation noise and prior error are correlated.

gain_gap proves that if Kopt S=B and S is symmetric, the covariance gap is
(K-Kopt) S (K-Kopt)^T. gain_gap_positive_semidefinite makes the corresponding
Loewner-order statement for PSD S. This is modeled linear covariance, not
actual nonlinear estimation MSE. Equally modeled optimal gains can tie.

conditional_factor_identity assumes the representation
P=L L^T, C=L U, R=U^T U+V V^T. For F=I-KH, the posterior becomes
(F L-K U^T)(F L-K U^T)^T+(K V)(K V)^T.
conditional_factor_positive_semidefinite proves its PSD property for any K.
The theorem assumes valid factors; it does not verify a floating-point solver.

With invertible L, an implementation obtains U by solving L U=C and checks
the conditional measurement covariance R-U^T U. Positive innovation S alone
is insufficient: impossible_scalar_joint gives P=H=R=1, C=2, S=6 and a
negative posterior variance. Invalid joint covariances must be rejected.

GNC.Estimation.InertialAidingObservability models body-frame optical-flow
rates, range above flat ground, and a constant local magnetic field. For its
physical interpretation assume positive height, known rotation conventions,
fixed ground and field, and position-independent inertial dynamics.

horizontal_translation_unobservable proves that any horizontal translation
leaves this observation unchanged. prediction_preserves_translation proves
the same symmetry for a constant-acceleration position prediction.
gps_observes_translation proves that a direct position observation separates
nonzero translations. These results describe an information limitation, not
a guarantee of a particular filter's observability consistency or recovery.
They exclude mapped terrain, spatial magnetic maps, landmarks and other
position-dependent aiding. An initial prior can still anchor an estimate.

Both modules are exported through GNC.Core. Their intended application is the
separately maintained CogniPilot Modelica ESKF and its generated-C adversarial
tests. There is no formal refinement from those implementations to Lean here.

Qualification for this change: Lean 4.29.1, targeted source recompilation of
ten estimation modules using the pinned checked dependency cache, project
kernel axiom audit, and scripts/check_structure.py. The audit permits only
propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; no admitted project theorem or
project axiom is permitted. This is not a full release verification run.
